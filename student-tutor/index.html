Questions you might have before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating a profile and chatting with learners costs $0. A 6% platform fee applies to completed sessions only.

How much do I actually keep?

The fee is 6% for the tutor and 6% for the learner. On a $100 session you keep $94 and the learner pays $106.

When do I get paid?

Learners pay up front and SOV-O holds the money in escrow. It is released when both you and the learner mark the session complete.

Will I get bookings?

We can't guarantee bookings. SOV-O gives your skills a place to be discovered, and learners are joining in Toronto now.

What is a Founding Tutor?

The first 20 tutors to join are featured on SOV-O's social media. We never post without your consent, and you approve before anything goes live.

What if a learner doesn't show up or disputes a session?

Check section 8 of our Terms and Conditions: CANCELLATIONS, RESCHEDULING, CONFLICTS AND REFUNDS

Can I tutor online as well as in person?

Yes you can chat with the learner and choose a format that works for both of you. Or apply for opportunities that specifically ask for online tutoring.

Do I need a degree or experience?

Sov-o gives a platform to both certified and experience based professionals. Showcase your skills and experience in your portfolio.

Can I tutor if I'm an international student?

Eligible international students may work within their work-authorization and weekly-hour limits. Check your study permit conditions before you start.

What do I choose when I sign up?

"Sign up as Opportunity Seeker" if you want to tutor. "Opportunity Poster" is for people looking for help.

In case you have more questions, reach out to us at info@sov-o.com and we would be happy to assist.
