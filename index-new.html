Inside the app

Built for Both Sides of the Job

Whether you are hiring or looking for work, everything happens in the same place.

Post an opportunity, or go find one

Choose your mode when you sign up. Post what you need and let people come to you, or browse live opportunities near you and apply in a couple of taps.

  • Filter by distance so you only see work you can actually reach
  • Set the pay and the timeframe when you post
  • Switch between posting and seeking whenever you like

Show your skills, and prove them

This is what makes SOV-O different. Upload certificates against the skills you are qualified in, and list hands-on experience separately, so people know exactly what they are getting.

  • Skill-Certified for formal qualifications you can evidence
  • Other Skill for experience earned on the job
  • Ratings and reviews build up as you complete work

Agree in chat, get paid to your wallet

Message before you commit, send or review a written offer, and pay through the app. Money sits safely in escrow until the job is marked complete, then lands in the wallet.

  • Offers show the full breakdown, fees and tax included
  • Payments handled by Stripe with escrow built in
  • Get notified the moment funds are released
