Requesting deletion of your SOV-O data

You can request deletion of your SOV-O account and the personal information associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

Email info@sov-o.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject “Data deletion request”. Include the email address or phone number linked to your SOV-O account so we can locate it.

What we delete

Your profile, account details, messages, and your opportunity and application history.

What we retain

As set out in Section 7 of our Privacy Policy, we may retain certain transaction and payment records where required by applicable law or for accounting and reporting purposes, and anonymised or aggregated data that can no longer be associated with you. Payment information is held by Stripe under their own retention policies.

Timeframe

We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.
