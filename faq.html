SOV-O is an online marketplace application that connects skilled professionals with individuals or businesses looking for help in various fields, including handyman services, driving, cooking, volunteering, and more. Whether you're seeking work or looking for someone to assist you, SOV-O makes it easy to find the right match.

SOV-O operates as a two-sided platform:

  • For Opportunity Posters: Post your Opportunities (e.g., handyman, driver, chef) and browse profiles of available professionals. Once you find a match, you can hire them directly through the platform.
  • For Opportunity Seekers: Create a profile showcasing your skills and availability. Browse opportunities posted by clients and apply to the ones that suit you.

Yes, creating an account on SOV-O is free. However, there may be fees for certain premium features or services, such as featured listings for clients or advanced search filters for professionals. All fees will be clearly outlined before you proceed.

SOV-O has an in-built payment processing system powered by Stripe as well as a wallet feature including Escrow payment facility which ensures that the money is only released once the opportunity gets completed.

Once you accept an offer, you pay through the app rather than in cash. The amount is taken at the point you accept and held securely until the work is marked complete, then released to the seeker's wallet. Card payments are handled by Stripe, so your card details are never stored by SOV-O.

Money you pay for an opportunity is held in escrow rather than going straight to the seeker. It stays there while the work is carried out, and is only released once the job is confirmed as complete. If something goes wrong before then, the payment has not left the platform, so there is a record of it and our support team can look into it with you.
