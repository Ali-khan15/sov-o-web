About Us

Welcome to SOV-O , Connecting Skills That Match Your Needs . Whether it’s fixing a leaky faucet, assembling furniture, organizing community events, or volunteering at a local shelter, our platform is designed to connect those who need help with those who can provide it—professionally or altruistically.

How It Works

Advanced tools for seamless job posting, hiring, payments, and communication

Opportunity Seekers
1. Create Profile

Sign up, select your certified or experienced skills, and for volunteers, choose places of interest. Add photos to showcase your work.

2. Find Opportunities

Browse or wait for posters to contact you based on your profile.

3. Apply or Connect

Apply to opportunities or respond to invitations.

4. Agree on Terms

Confirm project details and expectations with the poster.

5. Send Offer & Secure Payment

Submit your personalized offer. Payment is held safely in the app until work is done.

6. Complete Work & Get Paid

Finish the work, upload proof, and once approved, receive payment in your wallet.

Opportunity Posters
1. Create Account

Register and set up your profile to access skilled seekers and volunteers.

2. Post or Search

Post your opportunity or browse seeker profiles to find the right fit.

3. Review & Connect

Review offers or reach out to candidates directly.

4. Finalize Terms

Agree on scope, timeline, and deliverables with the seeker.

5. Accept Offer & Pay Securely

Accept the offer and pay through the app; funds are held until work is complete.

6. Confirm Completion & Release Payment

Approve the finished work and release payment to the seeker.
