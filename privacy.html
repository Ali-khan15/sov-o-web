Our Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last modified: April 2025

We at SOV-O Ventures Inc. (“SOV-O”, "company", "we", “us” or “our”) respect your privacy and data security and are committed to protecting it by complying with this policy.

This policy describes:

  • The types of information we may collect or that SOV-O Platform users and customers ("you") may provide when you use our services through our website (www.sov-o.com (the “Website”) or download, install, register with, access, or use the SOV-O App (the "App") (collectively, the “Services”).
  • Our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.
  • How we use personal information, and the circumstances under which we may share this information with a third party.

We will only use your personal information in accordance with this policy unless otherwise required by applicable law. We take steps to ensure that the personal information that we collect about you is adequate, relevant, not excessive, and used for limited purposes.

Privacy laws in Canada generally define "personal information" as any information about an identifiable individual, which includes information that can be used on its own or with other information to identify, contact, or locate a single person. Personal information does not include business contact information, including your name, title, or business contact information.

This policy applies only to information we collect through your use of the Services on our Website, our App, in email, and other electronic communications sent through or in connection with the Services.

This policy DOES NOT apply to information that:

  • We collect offline or on any other SOV-O platforms, apps or websites, including websites you may access through our Services.
  • You provide to or is collected by any third party (see Third-Party Information Collection).

Our Services, and any third parties, may have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read before providing information on or through them.

Please read this policy carefully to understand our policies and practices for collecting, processing, and storing your information. IF YOU DO NOT AGREE WITH OUR POLICIES AND PRACTICES, DO NOT ENGAGE WITH THE SERVICES, INCLUDING ACCESSING THE WEBSITE OR DOWNLOADING, REGISTERING WITH, OR USING THE APP. BY ENGAGING WITH THE SERVICES, ACCESSING THE WEBSITE, OR DOWNLOADING, REGISTERING WITH, OR USING THE APP, YOU INDICATE THAT YOU UNDERSTAND, ACCEPT, AND CONSENT TO THE PRACTICES DESCRIBED IN THIS PRIVACY POLICY.

This policy may change from time to time. Your continued use of the Services through the Website or the App after we make changes indicates that you accept and consent to those changes, so please check the policy periodically for updates.

Definitions:

  • “Opportunity Seeker” means any individual or entity that offers to perform services or Opportunities through the Services.
  • “Opportunity Poster” means any individual or entity that posts Opportunity Services for an Opportunity Seeker or Volunteer to perform through the Services.
  • “Opportunity Services” means any service posted by an Opportunity Poster or any service performed by an Opportunity Seeker.
  • “User(s)” means any Opportunity Poster, Opportunity Seeker, Volunteer, or any other person or entity using the Services.

All other capitalized terms used but not defined above shall have the meanings ascribed to them elsewhere in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect About You and How We Collect It

We collect information about you through:

  • Direct interactions with you when you provide it to us, for example, by filling in electronic forms, corresponding with us, and submitting your information required to make a user account and engage with our services, such as providing or obtaining an Opportunity Service.
  • Interactions between Opportunity Seekers, Opportunity Posters, and Volunteers, to ensure your safety and for quality assurance purposes we may collect information and data related to the interactions between users providing Opportunity Service(s) (Opportunity Seeker(s)), users receiving Opportunity Service(s) (Opportunity Poster(s)), and those providing Volunteer Services (Volunteer(s)).
  • Automated technologies or interactions, when you use the App, for example, usage details, IP addresses, and information collected through cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies.

The types of information that we collect include:

  • Personal information that we can reasonably use to directly or indirectly identify you, such as your name, mailing address, email address, telephone number, internet protocol (IP) address, user name or other similar identifier, billing and account information, geolocation, and any other identifier we may use to contact you ("personal information"). We provide an opportunity for any user to unsubscribe or opt-out of contact for marketing purposes or user feedback on an ongoing basis by using the unsubscribe mechanism at the bottom of our emails, or sending us an email with your intention to unsubscribe to info@sov-o.com.
  • ID and Verification for Opportunity Seekers: For safety and quality assurance purposes, we may require users to present valid forms of identification, such as government photo ID or professional certificates, to verify the authenticity of Opportunity Seekers.
  • Non-personal information that does not directly or indirectly reveal your identity or directly relate to an identified individual, such as demographic information or statistical or aggregated information. We may derive non-personal statistical or aggregated data from personal information. For example, we may aggregate personal information to calculate the percentage of users accessing a specific app feature.
  • Access to messages between Opportunity Seekers, Opportunity Posters, and Volunteers for your safety and quality assurance purposes.
  • Technical information, including your login information, device type, time zone setting, and usage details.

Depending on your age and privacy requirements in your country or jurisdiction, we may require a copy of your ID document for verification purposes. Any such ID will strictly be used for the verification event and will be immediately deleted after the verification has been processed.

Automatic Information Collection and Tracking Technologies

When you interact with the Services, including the App, it may automatically collect:

  • Usage details: When you access and use the Platform, we may automatically collect certain details of your access to and use of the Platform, including traffic data, location data, logs, and other communication data and the resources that you access and use on or through the Platform.
  • Device information: We may collect information about your mobile device and internet connection, including the device's unique device identifier, IP address, operating system, browser type, mobile network information, and the device's telephone number.
  • Stored information and files: The Platform may also access metadata and other information associated with other files stored on your device.
  • Location information: The App can collect real-time information about the location of your device. This feature allows us to provide you with a more tailored experience, including matching you to other users who may be within your geographical vicinity for group fitness sessions.

The technologies we use for this automatic data collection may include:

  • Cookies (or mobile cookies): A cookie is a small file placed on your mobile device. It may be possible to refuse to accept mobile cookies by activating the appropriate setting on your smartphone. However, if you select this setting, you may be unable to access certain parts of our App.
  • Web beacons: Pages of the App may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags and single-pixel gifs) that permit SOV-O, for example, to count users who have visited those pages and for other related app statistics (for example, recording the popularity of certain app content and verifying system and server integrity).

If you do not want us to collect this information, do not engage with the Services, download the App, and delete the App from your device. Note, however, that opting out of the Service’s collection of location information will cause its location-based features to be disabled, which will render certain features of the Services unusable.
